# PourLab design sources

## Selected OBSIDIAN direction — inspected 21 October 2026

The user selected OBSIDIAN after trying two isolated, working design alternatives. Professional references were inspected beyond their cover images before implementation. The integrated product adapts principles through original geometry and licensed dependencies; a gallery listing does not license its artwork, branding, animation or source code.

| Source actually inspected | Observed mechanism | Decision in PourLab |
|---|---|---|
| [Board — Brand Identity & Web Design, Luciana Botines](https://www.behance.net/gallery/223777003/Board-Brand-Identity-Web-Design), published May 2025 | Physical token imagery against a quiet black/lilac field; strong display typography; game identity, UI applications and a coherent brand system. Full modules beyond the cover were inspected. | A dark game stage, original physical tube geometry, compact condensed headline, limited lime action signal and bottom transport. Board's tokens, artwork, logo, wording, exact palette and code are not reused. |
| [Form + Fidget — Noodle Studio / Doug Alberts](https://www.behance.net/gallery/222516693/Form-Fidget), published 14 April 2025; [live composition](https://www.noodleanimation.com/form-and-fidget) | The full presentation and publicly embedded Rive interaction were opened. Dragging an orange endpoint changed a black curve: actual input-responsive motion, not inferred movement from a still. | Informed the contrasting FIDGET prototype and the principle that movement should respond to input. OBSIDIAN was selected instead. No Noodle artwork, Rive file/runtime, animation, logo or code is bundled. |

The selected product has a compact horizontal headline, a wide live stage and bottom action dock, rather than the FIDGET alternative's flat poster split. Its state-linked movement is original Three.js geometry. It does not reproduce the inspected Rive effect or claim fluid physics. The direction studies and private research captures remain in the job-hunt project's `output/behance-art-direction-2026-10-21/lab/pourlab/` folder; they are research records, not public production assets or user-research evidence.

### Active licensed implementation supply

- **Three.js 0.180.0 / r180**, vendored locally under MIT. The [official pinned sources and packaging change](../dist/vendor/three/README.md) and complete [MIT notice](../dist/vendor/three/LICENSE) are included. `stage3d.js` imports those modules for actual tubes and colored units; the renderer uses plain JavaScript and static hosting. No runtime CDN, React, paid service, API key or account is needed.
- **Barlow Condensed Bold**, [official Google Fonts source](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/barlowcondensed/BarlowCondensed-Bold.ttf), unmodified 109,912-byte file. Copyright 2017 The Barlow Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1; full notice retained as `dist/assets/BarlowCondensed-OFL.txt`. Used for the current display type.
- **Manrope**, [official Google Fonts source](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/manrope/Manrope%5Bwght%5D.ttf), unmodified variable font with its full `dist/assets/OFL.txt`. Copyright 2018 The Manrope Project Authors, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Used for current controls and body copy. Both fonts are hosted locally; their official upstream sources/notices were inspected and permit redistribution with those notices.
- The 2D board, rule diagrams, tube geometry, numeric textures, original `p↗` emblem, controller adapter and bounded transitions are implementation work for PourLab. Existing MIT-licensed pure rules, worker, portable formats and production persistence remain in use. No gallery artwork, model, video, commercial font or designer private material is redistributed.

Kenney's free CC0 UI Pack and the MIT-licensed Motion library were checked during sourcing but not incorporated: sprite controls did not improve the selected composition, and the bounded state-linked transitions did not justify another runtime. No designer was contacted, no asset/service was purchased, and no private project data was uploaded for this direction. Active and historical files are distinguished in [asset provenance](../dist/assets/SOURCES.md).

The [selected-design browser record](evidence/SELECTED-DESIGN-QA.md) evaluates the integrated product. Reference inspection, license checks and unit tests do not establish player demand, complete accessibility or acceptance on every hardware device.

## Superseded Cosmos instrument direction

Historical revision recorded 21 October 2026. The user requested a new direction because PourLab and QuoteLens looked too similar. It replaced the prior Lifemed white/black/glass constraint and was later superseded by the play-first revision and then OBSIDIAN. The sections below describe that earlier instrument presentation.

The public Cosmos Explore page, a single search for “playful interface”, and the following two full element pages were inspected visually in a dedicated browser tab. The full artwork, not just a result title, informed the decisions. No account was created, and no login/quota gate was bypassed.

### Historical product fit and deliberate contrast

PourLab's central colored board and persistent mode rail were intended to invite experimentation; the console separated editing from analysis. QuoteLens handled source-linked purchasing decisions. This earlier PourLab revision used condensed display typography, a saturated stage, a full-width board, rounded physical controls and state-linked game motion rather than the preceding white glass hero and purchasing-workbench grammar.

Actual browser interaction, reduced motion, keyboard, mobile and search-state checks must be evaluated on the integrated product; reference notes alone are not acceptance evidence.
